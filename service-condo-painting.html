Condo Painting

Our Approach

Condo Painting in Toronto & the GTA

Painting a condo isn't the same as painting a house. Building rules, elevator access windows, restricted working hours, hallway protection requirements, and neighbouring units all add layers of coordination that require experience to manage smoothly. We've done it many times — and we handle every detail.

Cornerstone Pro Painting works in condo buildings across Toronto and the GTA, from smaller boutique buildings to large high-rise towers. We book elevator time with building management, lay proper hallway protection, work within permitted hours, and leave your unit — and the building's common areas — exactly as we found them. Clean work, no surprises.

How We Work

Our Process

01
Building Coordination

We contact building management to confirm working hours, elevator booking windows, and any specific building requirements before we start — so there are no surprises on the day.

02
Protection Setup

Elevator pads go up, hallway carpet protection is laid, and all surfaces inside the unit are covered. We treat the common areas with the same care as your unit.

03
Prep, Prime & Paint

All surfaces are patched, sanded, and primed before paint goes on. We work ceiling to floor — ceilings, walls, trim, and doors — for a consistent, professional finish throughout.

04
Cleanup & Walkthrough

All protection materials are removed, common areas are left spotless, and we walk through the unit with you before we leave. No mess, no damage — guaranteed.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Condo Painting

Do you handle elevator booking with the building?

Yes. We coordinate directly with building management to reserve elevator time and confirm any required protection measures. You don't need to manage that yourself.

Can you work within my building's permitted hours?

Absolutely. We always work within the building's approved hours — whether that's weekday daytime only or a specific window. We confirm the schedule before booking your job.

Can you paint the condo while I'm at work?

Yes. Many of our condo clients hand off a key or arrange access through the building concierge. We're comfortable working independently and will send you a photo update when the job is done.

Do you protect the hallway and elevator?

Yes — always. We lay hallway protection pads and elevator padding as required by building management. We treat common areas with the same care as your unit.

How long does a typical condo painting project take?

A one-bedroom condo typically takes one to two days. A two-bedroom or larger unit is usually two to three days. We'll give you a precise timeline in your written estimate.

Do you paint ceilings and trim as well?

Yes. Every condo painting project includes ceiling, walls, trim, and doors unless you specify otherwise. We can also quote individual rooms if you only need certain areas done.
