Our Process

How the Walkthrough Works

Every project ends the same way: a structured, unhurried review of the finished work with you present. Here is exactly what happens before we consider any job complete.

01
Pre-Walkthrough Check

Before we call you in, our crew does their own internal review — checking lines, coverage, and surface consistency throughout the space. We catch and correct issues before you ever see them.

02
Walk With Us

A senior member of our crew walks every painted surface with you. You look at whatever you want, for as long as you need. There is no rush and no pressure to sign off before you are satisfied.

03
Immediate Fixes

Any touch-ups identified during the walkthrough are completed before we pack up. Most are resolved on the spot within minutes — we always keep materials on hand for exactly this reason.

04
24-Hour Return Guarantee

If something surfaces after the walkthrough — a spot you missed, something that looks different in daylight — we return within 24 hours to address it, at no charge. Your satisfaction does not expire when we leave the driveway.

Our Warranty

What Our 2-Year Painting Warranty Covers

Our painting warranty Toronto homeowners can rely on covers every aspect of our workmanship for two full years after project completion. If we painted it and it fails, we fix it — free of charge, no argument.

Peeling & Bubbling

If paint peels or bubbles on surfaces we prepared and painted within 2 years, we return and fix it free. No service call fee, no diagnostic visit — just a repair, scheduled promptly.

Edge Failures

If masked edges separate or crack within the warranty period, it’s covered. Clean, sharp lines are a hallmark of professional work — and we stand behind them for two full years.

Adhesion Issues

Any failure in surface adhesion attributable to our application or preparation is our responsibility to rectify. Proper prep is the foundation of a lasting paint job — we take full ownership of ours.

Touch-Up Access

Even beyond the warranty period, we provide touch-up paint specifications so your home can be maintained perfectly. You will always know the exact product, sheen, and colour code used on your project.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Our Painting Guarantee in Toronto

What if I find something wrong after you’ve left?

Call or email us. If it’s within the warranty period and attributable to our workmanship, we schedule a return visit — usually within 24–48 hours — and fix it at no charge.

What does your 2-year warranty cover?

Any defect in workmanship including peeling, cracking, bubbling, or adhesion failure on surfaces we prepared and painted. It does not cover damage from external causes — water intrusion, structural movement, or intentional damage.

Do you fix mistakes the same day?

If an issue is identified during the final walkthrough, yes — we address it before we leave. Issues discovered afterwards are typically scheduled within 24–48 hours.

What if I’m not happy with the colour after the job is done?

Colour change requests after the work is complete are not covered under our warranty — this would be a new scope of work. That’s why we encourage colour samples and consultation before we begin painting. We’re happy to assist with colour selection upfront.

Do all painting companies offer a final walkthrough?

No. Many contractors complete the job and send an invoice without any formal handover. Our walkthrough process is one of the ways we hold ourselves to a higher standard.
