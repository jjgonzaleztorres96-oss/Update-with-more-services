Dining Room Painting in Toronto — Colour That Sets the Table

Dining rooms lend themselves to bolder colour choices than other rooms in the home. They are typically used in the evening under controlled lighting, which means rich, saturated colours — deep navy, forest green, warm terracotta, saturated burgundy — perform particularly well here. The right colour in a dining room creates an atmosphere that elevates every meal served in it.

Cornerstone Pro Painting helps Toronto homeowners think through the dining room colour decision carefully — considering the existing furniture, flooring, lighting fixtures, and adjacent spaces — before a brush touches the wall. We have painted dining rooms across the full spectrum of Toronto's housing stock, from compact semi-detached dining rooms to large formal dining spaces in Rosedale and Forest Hill.

Our Process — Step by Step

Step 1: Colour Consultation — We discuss the mood you want to create, the fixed elements in the room (floor, furniture, fixtures), and help you arrive at a colour that will work beautifully in your specific dining room.

Step 2: Preparation — Table and chairs moved and protected, china cabinets and sideboards covered, floors protected, surfaces cleaned and patched.

Step 3: Priming — Dark colour changes and significant repair areas are primed before finish coats to ensure full coverage and colour accuracy.

Step 4: Ceiling Treatment — Ceiling painted first — in white, off-white, or a lighter tint of the wall colour if a tonal effect is desired.

Step 5: Walls & Wainscoting — Two full coats on all walls. If the room has wainscoting or chair rail, these are painted separately in the appropriate sheen.

Step 6: Trim & Doors — All trim, casings, and doors finished cleanly to complete the room.

Frequently Asked Questions

What colours work best in a dining room?

Richer, more saturated colours tend to perform well — deep navy, forest green, warm burgundy, terracotta. These colours create atmosphere in the evening lighting that dining rooms are typically used in. We help you select a specific shade that works with your furniture and flooring.

How long does dining room painting take?

A standard dining room takes one day. Rooms with wainscoting, coffered ceilings, or extensive trim may take one and a half to two days.

Can you paint the wainscoting a different colour?

Yes. Two-tone treatments with wainscoting in a different colour or sheen than the upper walls are one of our most popular dining room requests.

What sheen do you recommend for dining room walls?

Eggshell is our standard recommendation for dining room walls. It provides a slightly cleanable surface and hides imperfections better than satin, while looking richer than flat paint under the artificial lighting that dining rooms typically use.

What does dining room painting cost in Toronto?

A standard dining room runs $700–$1,200. Rooms with wainscoting or extensive trim work run higher. Written quote provided after walkthrough.

Do dark colours make a dining room feel smaller?

Not necessarily. In a room used primarily in the evening with ambient and accent lighting, a dark colour can make the space feel more intimate and defined rather than small. We help you evaluate this during the colour consultation.
