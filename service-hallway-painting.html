Our Approach

Hallway Painting in Toronto & the GTA

Hallways and foyers take more traffic than any other space in a home — scuffs, fingerprints, marks from bags and furniture, and general daily wear show up faster here than anywhere else. The right paint for a hallway isn't just about colour — it's about a sheen that cleans easily and a product that holds up over time.

Cornerstone Pro Painting handles hallway and foyer painting for all home sizes — narrow apartment corridors, entry foyers with double-height ceilings, and long hallway runs connecting multiple rooms. We select products specifically suited for high-traffic walls, prep surfaces thoroughly, and work efficiently to minimize disruption to the household.

How We Work

Our Process

01
Surface Preparation

High-traffic hallway walls often have scuffs, marks, and chipped paint at corners and edges. We address all damage before painting begins.

02
Corner & Edge Protection

Corners in hallways take the most damage. We apply corner bead compound where needed and prime all repaired areas before topcoat.

03
Durable Topcoat Application

We use satin or semi-gloss finishes in hallways — higher sheen means better durability and easier cleaning in high-contact areas.

04
Trim & Door Painting

Baseboards, door casings, and doors in hallways are included. A clean trim line makes the whole space look sharper.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Hallway Painting

What paint finish is best for hallways?

Satin or semi-gloss. Hallways see more contact and abrasion than most rooms, so a higher-sheen finish — which cleans more easily — is the right choice.

Can you paint a stairwell at the same time as the hallway?

Yes — stairwell walls are typically included in hallway painting projects. We work with ladders and extension equipment to reach high walls safely.

How long does hallway painting take?

A typical hallway takes half a day to a full day. Larger entry foyers with high ceilings or stairwell walls extending up two storeys may take longer.

Can you paint just the hallway without doing any other rooms?

Absolutely. Single-room and single-area projects are a regular part of our work. We'll complete the hallway as a standalone project with a full written estimate.

Do you match the hallway colour to the adjacent rooms?

We can — or you can choose a contrasting colour for the hallway. We'll ensure clean colour transitions at doorways and room openings.
