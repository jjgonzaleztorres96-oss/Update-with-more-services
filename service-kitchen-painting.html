Kitchen Painting in Toronto — More Demanding Than It Looks

Kitchen walls are the hardest interior surface to paint well and have last. The combination of cooking grease, steam from pots, food splatter, and repeated cleaning creates conditions that destroy standard paint faster than almost anything else in the home. Getting a kitchen paint job right means thorough degreasing before any paint is applied, the right product selection (a scrubbable, durable finish), and application technique that accounts for the complex geometry of a kitchen — around upper and lower cabinets, above the range, behind the refrigerator, around the exhaust hood.

Cornerstone Pro Painting has been painting Toronto kitchens since 2009 and we understand what this surface demands. Our kitchen paint jobs start with a professional degreasing that removes the layer of cooking residue that most homeowners don't realize is on their walls — and that, if not removed, causes paint adhesion failure within months.

Our Process — Step by Step

Step 1: Surface Degreasing — Kitchen walls are cleaned with a commercial degreaser to remove cooking residue, grease, and any other contaminants that would prevent paint adhesion. This step is non-negotiable.

Step 2: Preparation — All appliances masked or moved where possible. Cabinets, countertops, and backsplash carefully masked. Holes patched and sanded.

Step 3: Priming — Previously stained areas and any bare patches are primed with a stain-blocking primer.

Step 4: Painting — Two full coats of a scrubbable, semi-gloss or satin finish product are applied in the correct order — ceiling first, then walls, working around the cabinet geometry.

Step 5: Trim & Detail Work — All trim, window casings, and the area under the upper cabinets are finished cleanly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to empty the kitchen?

No. We work around your appliances and ask that countertops be cleared before we arrive. We mask cabinets, countertops, and the backsplash carefully before any painting begins.

What paint finish is best for kitchen walls?

Satin or semi-gloss. These sheens provide the washability that kitchen walls need. Flat or eggshell paint in a kitchen will mark and stain quickly and cannot be properly cleaned.

Can you paint just the walls without the cabinets?

Yes. Kitchen wall painting and cabinet painting are separate services and are quoted independently. Many homeowners paint the walls with us first and address the cabinets in a subsequent project.

Do I need to remove the refrigerator and range?

Not necessarily. We can paint behind the refrigerator if it is on wheels and can be rolled out easily. The area behind a built-in range is typically left if the appliance cannot be safely moved.

What does kitchen painting cost in Toronto?

Kitchen walls (excluding cabinets) typically run $700–$1,100 depending on the size and configuration of the kitchen. Written quote provided after walkthrough.

How do I prevent the kitchen paint from peeling again?

Proper degreasing before painting is the most important factor. Most kitchen paint failures trace back to inadequate surface preparation, not paint quality. Our degreasing process addresses this at the outset of every kitchen job.
