Kitchen Painting

Our Approach

Kitchen Painting in Toronto & the GTA

Kitchen walls take more abuse than almost any other surface in a home — grease, steam, splashes, and daily contact all take a toll. A professional kitchen paint job uses the right products for the environment: higher-sheen finishes that clean easily, primers that seal surface stains, and topcoats that hold up to moisture and heat over time.

We handle kitchen painting for all kitchen sizes and styles — from galley kitchens in condos to large open-concept kitchen-dining spaces in detached homes. We work around existing appliances and cabinetry, protect countertops and floors throughout, and use low-VOC products so the kitchen is usable again quickly.

How We Work

Our Process

01
Surface Assessment

Kitchen walls often have grease buildup, stains, or previous paint that hasn't adhered well. We assess the surface condition and prep accordingly.

02
Cleaning & Priming

Surfaces are cleaned, degreased if needed, and primed with a stain-blocking primer before any topcoat is applied.

03
Wall & Trim Painting

We apply premium satin or semi-gloss topcoats — finishes that are durable, washable, and suited to the kitchen environment.

04
Appliance & Cabinet Protection

All appliances, countertops, and cabinetry are covered and protected throughout the job. Nothing gets paint on it that shouldn't.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Kitchen Painting

What paint finish is best for kitchen walls?

Satin is our most common recommendation for kitchen walls — it's washable, durable, and doesn't look too shiny. Semi-gloss is appropriate for high-traffic areas or if you prefer a more reflective finish.

Can you paint around appliances without moving them?

Yes. We work carefully around built-in appliances. Freestanding appliances can be moved slightly to allow access to the wall behind them.

Do you paint kitchen ceilings?

Yes — kitchen ceiling painting can be included in the scope. We use flat white or a slightly tinted ceiling colour depending on your preference.

How long does kitchen painting take?

Most kitchen painting projects take one day. Larger kitchen-dining open spaces or kitchens with extensive trim may take a day and a half.

Can you paint my kitchen after I've had cabinets installed?

Yes. Many clients repaint after a cabinet installation or replacement. We protect the new cabinetry completely and work around it carefully.
