Our Process

What On-Time Service Looks Like, From Quote to Completion

Reliability isn’t a single moment — it is a sequence of kept promises. Here is exactly what to expect when you book with Cornerstone Pro Painting as your on-time painters in Toronto.

01
Written Estimate Within 12 Hours

Submit an inquiry and you will have a detailed, itemized written estimate in your inbox within 12 hours. No waiting days for a callback, no vague verbal quotes. You know the scope, the cost, and the timeline before you commit to anything.

02
Start Date Confirmed in Your Estimate Document

Your estimate is not just a price — it includes your confirmed start date in writing. We block that date on our calendar immediately. You will not receive a follow-up call weeks later asking if you are still interested or whether a different date works better for us.

03
24-Hour Courtesy Reminder Before Crew Arrives

The day before your project begins, you will receive a confirmation message from us with the arrival time, the crew lead’s name, and any final prep instructions. No surprises, no guessing. You can plan your morning around a specific window, not a four-hour range.

04
Project Completed Within the Agreed Timeline

We finish within the timeframe stated in your estimate — or we explain why in writing and provide a new confirmed completion date. When we walk out, the job is done, the site is clean, and we have done a final walkthrough with you to confirm every surface meets our standard.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
On-Time Painting in Toronto

How do you guarantee on-time arrival?

We calendar every job before confirming the start date and never double-book crews. You receive a 24-hour reminder the day before work begins.

What happens if there’s a delay?

Weather or unforeseen site conditions occasionally affect exterior work. In those cases, you’ll hear from us the same day — never after the fact — and we’ll provide a new confirmed date immediately.

How far in advance should I book?

Interior painting can typically be scheduled within 1–2 weeks. Exterior projects in peak season (May–September) are often 2–4 weeks out. We recommend booking early to secure your preferred date.

Do your crews work on weekends?

Yes. We schedule weekend work when it fits the client’s preference. Weekend rates are the same as weekday rates — no premium.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens. We ask for 48 hours’ notice to move a booking. We’ll find the next available date that works for you without any fees.
