What You Get

What’s Included in Our Estimate

Full Scope of Work

Every surface to be painted is listed explicitly. Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, cabinets — nothing is vague. You know exactly what is and isn’t included before a single brush touches your walls.

Product Specifications

We specify the brand, product line, and sheen for every surface. Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, or equivalent premium brands — documented in writing so you can verify what goes on your home.

Timeline & Start Date

Our estimate includes the expected project duration and your proposed start date — not “sometime in the next few weeks.” You plan around a real schedule, not a guess.

Fixed Total Price

The number in the estimate is the number you pay. No surprise labour charges, no “we ran into an issue” add-ons after the fact. The price is locked before work begins.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Our Painting Estimates

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. No travel charge, no consultation fee, no obligation. We provide estimates as a standard part of how we do business. Whether you book with us or not, there is no cost to receiving your written quote.

How quickly will I receive my estimate?

Within 12 hours of your inquiry in most cases. If we need an on-site visit, we schedule that within 1–2 business days and still deliver the written estimate quickly after. We understand you have a project to plan.

Can I get a quote remotely without a site visit?

Yes, for most interior projects. Send us clear photos of the spaces — walls, ceilings, trim, and any areas of concern — and we can provide an accurate written estimate. Larger exterior projects or spaces with complex conditions may require an in-person assessment to ensure accuracy.

What if the price changes after work begins?

It doesn’t. Our estimate is a fixed price. If an unforeseen condition arises that requires additional work — structural issues behind drywall, for example — we stop and discuss it with you before proceeding. You approve any change in scope before it happens. There are no surprise invoices at the end.

How do your estimates compare to other painters?

Our estimates are more detailed than most. We specify products, number of coats, surfaces, and timeline. That level of detail protects you as the homeowner — there is no room for misinterpretation or post-job disputes about what was or wasn’t included.
