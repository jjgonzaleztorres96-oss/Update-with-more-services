Bedroom Painting in Toronto — Done Right

Bedroom painting seems straightforward, but the details matter significantly. The quality of the cut-in lines along the ceiling and baseboards, the consistency of the finish on the walls, the precision of the trim work around doors and window casings — these are the elements that determine whether a painted bedroom looks professionally done or like a weekend DIY project.

Cornerstone Pro Painting has been painting bedrooms across Toronto and the GTA since 2009. We use premium, low-VOC paints that are safe for the room where you spend a third of your life, and we work efficiently to minimize the disruption to your household. A standard bedroom typically takes one to two days from prep to final coat.

Our Process — Step by Step

Step 1: Consultation & Colour Selection — We discuss your goals, preferred palette, and any specific requirements — accent wall, closet interior, trim colour — and help you land on choices you'll love waking up to.

Step 2: Room Preparation — Furniture is moved and protected, floor is covered, switch plates and outlet covers are removed, and all surfaces are cleaned and patched before any paint is applied.

Step 3: Priming Where Needed — New drywall, repaired areas, and significant colour changes (especially dark-to-light) receive a proper primer coat before finish paint.

Step 4: Painting — Ceiling, Walls, Trim — We paint in the correct order: ceiling first, then walls, then trim and doors. Each surface receives two full coats with proper drying time between coats.

Step 5: Detail & Touch-Up — After the room dries, we inspect every edge and corner, address any imperfections, reinstall hardware, and leave the room ready to move back into.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does bedroom painting take?

A standard bedroom (under 200 sq ft) takes one day for prep, priming if needed, and two coats. Larger master bedrooms with extensive trim may take a day and a half to two days.

Do I need to empty the room?

No. Our crew moves furniture to the centre of the room and covers it. We ask that you remove fragile items, personal belongings from dressers and nightstands, and clear the floor area around the walls.

Can you paint the closet interior too?

Yes. Closet interiors are quoted separately but can be added to any bedroom painting job. Just let us know during the estimate walkthrough.

How long before I can sleep in the room?

With low-VOC latex paint, the room is safe to sleep in within 24 hours. We recommend keeping a window open for ventilation for the first night if possible.

What does bedroom painting cost in Toronto?

A standard bedroom runs $500–$900 including walls, ceiling, and trim. Master bedrooms with higher ceilings or more extensive trim work run $800–$1,400. We provide a written quote after the walkthrough.

Can you help me choose a colour?

Yes. Colour consultation is included in our estimate process. We help you think through your options based on your lighting, flooring, and furniture — and we always test samples on the wall before committing.
