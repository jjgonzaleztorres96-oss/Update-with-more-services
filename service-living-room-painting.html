Living Room Painting in Toronto — Making the Main Space Shine

The living room demands the most from a paint job. It is a high-visibility space with complex surfaces — often including crown moulding, accent walls, feature fireplaces, and large open layouts that reveal every lap mark and sheen inconsistency. Getting a living room right requires skill, the right product selection, and a crew that takes pride in their work.

Cornerstone Pro Painting has completed living room repaints across every kind of Toronto home — Victorian detached homes in the Annex with plaster walls and detailed moulding, open-concept condos in Liberty Village, and large family homes in Richmond Hill and Vaughan. We know what each type of space demands and we deliver it consistently.

Our Process — Step by Step

Step 1: Estimate Walkthrough — We assess the room — ceiling height, surface condition, trim complexity, crown moulding — and provide a written quote with clear scope.

Step 2: Colour Consultation — We help you select wall colour, trim colour, and ceiling treatment. We test samples on the actual wall before any work begins.

Step 3: Surface Preparation — Furniture protected, floors covered, surfaces cleaned, holes patched, crown moulding and trim carefully masked.

Step 4: Ceiling First — Ceiling is always painted first — flat paint or colour — to avoid dripping on finished walls.

Step 5: Walls & Feature Treatments — Walls painted in two full coats. Any accent wall or feature treatment is completed as part of the wall phase.

Step 6: Trim, Moulding & Doors — All trim, crown moulding, window casings, and doors are cut in cleanly and finished in the specified sheen.

Why Toronto Homeowners Choose Cornerstone Pro Painting

Crown moulding and trim precision is where living room paint jobs are won or lost. Our painters cut in moulding by hand — no tape shortcuts that leave ragged edges — and every transition between wall colour and trim is sharp and consistent. We use semi-gloss on all trim for durability and visual definition, and our wall finishes are rolled with consistent technique that leaves no lap marks under any lighting condition.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does living room painting take?

A standard living room takes one to two days. Large open-concept main floors or rooms with extensive crown moulding and trim may take two to three days.

Can you paint an accent wall a different colour?

Yes. Accent walls are included in the quote at no additional charge when they are in the same room as the main paint scope.

Do you paint crown moulding?

Yes. Crown moulding is included in our living room painting quotes. Cutting in crown moulding cleanly is one of the most technically demanding aspects of interior painting, and we do it well.

What finish do you use on living room walls?

We typically recommend eggshell for living room walls — it provides a slightly cleanable surface without the reflectivity of satin, and it hides minor imperfections better than satin under raking light. We discuss finish options during the estimate.

What does living room painting cost in Toronto?

A standard living room runs $800–$1,400. Large open-concept main floors run $1,600–$2,800. Written quote provided after walkthrough.

Can you match paint to my existing colour?

Yes. We can match any existing colour using a paint sample taken from the wall, or from a manufacturer's code if you have the original paint information.
