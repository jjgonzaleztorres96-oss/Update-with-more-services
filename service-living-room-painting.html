Our Approach

Living Room Painting in Toronto & the GTA

The living room is usually the most visible space in a home — the first thing guests see, and the room you spend the most time in. A professional paint job in this space makes a bigger impact than in any other room, and the difference between a rushed job and a careful one shows clearly.

We handle living room painting for all styles of homes — open-concept spaces with cathedral ceilings, traditional rooms with detailed crown moulding, and everything in between. Furniture is moved and protected, prep is thorough, and every line is taped cleanly. We don't leave until the room looks right.

How We Work

Our Process

01
On-Site Estimate

We assess the room — ceiling height, trim complexity, surface condition — and provide a written estimate with a full scope of work.

02
Furniture & Floor Protection

All furniture is moved where possible and protected with drop cloths. Floors are covered completely before any paint is applied.

03
Surface Prep

Patching, sanding, and priming are completed before painting begins. Any existing damage to walls or trim is addressed first.

04
Painting & Trim Work

Ceilings, walls, and trim are painted in sequence. Detailed trim and crown moulding receive careful brush work, not roller coverage.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Living Room Painting

How long does living room painting take?

Most living rooms take one to two days depending on ceiling height, trim complexity, and the number of colours. Open-concept spaces connected to a dining area may take longer.

Can you paint high ceilings and cathedral ceilings?

Yes. We work with extension poles and scaffolding where needed. High ceilings are part of our standard scope for living rooms.

Do you paint crown moulding and baseboards?

Yes — crown moulding, baseboards, window trim, and door casings are all included in the living room painting scope.

What's the best paint finish for living room walls?

Eggshell and satin are both popular choices for living rooms. Eggshell gives a softer look; satin is more durable and slightly more reflective.

Can I stay in the house while the living room is being painted?

Yes. We work with proper ventilation and low-VOC products. The room is typically accessible again the same evening or the next morning.
