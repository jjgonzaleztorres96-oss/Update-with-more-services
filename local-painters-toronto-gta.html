Who We Are

A Painting Company That Actually Knows Your Neighbourhood

Cornerstone Pro Painting is not a franchise. We are a locally owned and operated painting company that has been serving Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area since 2009. The owner is on-site. The crew lives in the communities we paint. When you call us, you reach the people doing the work — not a call centre routing your job to the next available subcontractor.

Direct Access to Decision Makers

When you hire a local painting company, you talk to the owner — not a dispatcher or a project coordinator. Questions get answered the same day. Changes get made in real time. You are never left waiting for someone with authority to call you back.

Accountability That Lasts

A local business has to live with its reputation. We can't disappear after a job — we're your neighbour, and we're working in your neighbourhood tomorrow. Our 2-year warranty isn't fine print; it's a promise backed by 15 years of standing behind our work.

Built on Referrals

More than 60% of our new clients come from referrals. That's what local reputation looks like — not paid ads or a franchise marketing budget. When your neighbour recommends us, that carries more weight than any review platform.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Our Local Painting Services in Toronto & the GTA

Where in the GTA does Cornerstone Pro Painting work?

We serve Toronto and the entire Greater Toronto Area, including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Markham, Oakville, and Burlington. Our crews are on the road across the GTA every day. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, give us a call — we'll tell you straight away.

Is Cornerstone Pro Painting locally owned?

Yes. We are a locally owned and operated painting business — not a franchise or a national chain. The owner is personally involved in every project, and our crew members are people who live in the communities we serve. That's not a marketing line; it's the reason we've been able to grow almost entirely through referrals for 15 years.

How long has Cornerstone Pro Painting been in business?

We've been painting homes and businesses in Toronto and the GTA since 2009 — over 15 years. That kind of longevity isn't accidental. It comes from delivering consistent work, standing behind it with a 2-year warranty, and treating every client like a neighbour. Because they usually are.

Why hire a local painter instead of a national chain?

Local painters know the neighbourhoods, the housing stock, and the clients personally. When you work with Cornerstone, you deal directly with the people doing the work. Questions get answered the same day. Issues get resolved without a support queue. And if something comes up after the job, we're a phone call away — not a franchise owner trying to navigate a corporate complaint process.

Do you do commercial painting in the GTA as well?

Yes. We handle residential and commercial painting across the GTA — offices, retail spaces, strata buildings, and multi-unit properties. If you have a commercial project, contact us and we'll scope it within 24 hours and provide a written estimate with no obligation.
