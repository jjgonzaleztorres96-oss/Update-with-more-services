The Site Visit

What Happens During a Free On-Site Painting Estimate

An on-site estimate is the only way to give you a number you can actually count on. We walk every space, take measurements, note wall conditions, assess prep requirements, and talk through your goals — colours, finish, timeline. By the time we leave, we have everything we need to put a written quote together.

There's no sales pressure during the visit. We're there to understand the scope and answer your questions honestly — including the ones other contractors dodge. If we think something isn't worth doing, we'll say so. If there's a smarter way to approach your project, we'll tell you.

What We Assess

What We Look at During the Visit

Wall & Surface Condition

We check for cracks, nail pops, water stains, peeling, or any surface that needs prep work before paint. These are factored into the quote upfront — no surprise add-ons mid-project.

Scope & Measurements

We measure the square footage of every surface being painted — walls, ceilings, trim, doors. Accurate measurements mean accurate pricing. You're not guessing, and neither are we.

Colour & Finish Consultation

Not sure what colour or sheen is right for the space? We advise on what works — flat vs. eggshell, light vs. dark, accent walls. No obligation to follow our suggestions, but most clients appreciate having a professional opinion on-site.

Timeline & Logistics

We discuss start dates, how long the project will take, what you need to do to prepare (if anything), and how the work will be scheduled around your household. No vague "a few days" — a real projected timeline.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
On-Site Painting Estimates in Toronto

Do I have to be home for the painting estimate?

Yes, for an on-site estimate we'll need access to the spaces. We schedule at your convenience — days, evenings, and weekends. If your schedule is tight, let us know and we'll work around it.

How long does an on-site painting estimate take?

Most residential visits take 30–60 minutes depending on the scope. We'll walk every room, measure what needs measuring, discuss your preferences, and answer all your questions before we leave.

Is the on-site visit really free?

Completely free, no obligation. We don't charge for estimates regardless of whether you book with us. The written quote is yours to keep and compare.

Can I get a painting estimate without a site visit?

Yes — for smaller projects like a single room or a bathroom, photos and measurements are often enough. We'll let you know after your initial inquiry which approach makes more sense for your project.

How soon after the visit will I receive the written estimate?

Within 24 hours — usually the same day. We don't sit on quotes. You'll receive a detailed written estimate with exact scope, price, and projected timeline as soon as we're back at the office.
