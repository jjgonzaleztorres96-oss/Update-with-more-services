How to Reach Us

Four Ways to Get Your Quote

Call or Text

Call or text 437-242-3829. We answer during business hours and respond to texts quickly. If you leave a voicemail, we call back the same day.

Online Form

Fill out the estimate form on our website. Tell us what you need, attach photos if you have them, and we'll respond with a written estimate or schedule a visit — whichever makes more sense for your project.

For smaller projects — a bedroom, a bathroom, a hallway — photos are often all we need. Snap the walls, note the dimensions, and we can turn around a written quote within 12 hours.

For full-house repaints, exterior projects, or anything complex, we'll come to you. Free on-site estimates, scheduled at your convenience — including evenings.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Getting a Painting Quote in Toronto

How do I get a painting quote in Toronto?

Call, text (437-242-3829), or fill out the online form. We respond within 12 hours — often the same day. For most residential projects we can provide a ballpark online; for larger or more complex jobs we'll schedule a free on-site visit.

Do I need to be home for the quote?

For an online estimate, no — just send photos of the spaces and measurements if you have them. For an on-site visit, yes, we'll need access. We schedule at your convenience, including evenings.

How long does it take to get a painting quote?

We turn around written estimates within 12–24 hours of the consultation. If you need something urgent, let us know — we can usually accommodate same-day turnaround for straightforward projects.

Is the painting quote really free and no obligation?

Yes, completely. We don't charge for estimates and we don't pressure you to book. The quote is yours to keep and compare — whether you hire us or not.

What information do I need to provide for a painting quote?

The more detail the better — room dimensions, current wall condition, paint preference (we can advise on this), and any specific concerns. Photos help a lot. If you're not sure, just reach out and we'll guide you through what we need.
