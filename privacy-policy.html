Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Cornerstone Pro Painting collects, how we use it, and how you can contact us with questions or requests.

Cornerstone Pro Painting · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Who We Are

Cornerstone Pro Painting is a professional painting company serving Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. Our business address is 3838 Bathurst St, North York, ON M6A 2E4. You can contact us at cornerstonepropainting@gmail.com or by phone at 437-242-3829.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily submit it through our free estimate request form. The information we collect includes:

  • Your name — first and last name, used to address your estimate
  • Phone number — used to follow up on your estimate request
  • Email address — used to send your written estimate
  • Project details — service type, number of rooms, timeline, current wall condition, and neighbourhood — used to prepare an accurate quote
  • Photos — if you choose to upload them, used to assess the job before providing a quote

We do not collect payment information, government identification, or any sensitive personal data.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Prepare and send you a free written estimate
  • Follow up with you by phone or email about your project
  • Answer questions about your request

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your information for any purpose beyond responding to your estimate request.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services in operating this website:

  • EmailJS — our estimate form submissions are transmitted via EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service. Your form data is sent through EmailJS to deliver your request to our inbox. EmailJS's privacy policy is available at their website.
  • Google Analytics 4 — we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website (pages visited, time on site, general location by region). Analytics only loads if you accept cookies through our cookie consent notice. We do not use analytics data to identify individual visitors. You can opt out at any time by declining cookies or using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google Fonts — this website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log the font requests as part of their service. No personal information from our forms is shared with Google Fonts.

Cookies

This website uses cookies only if you accept our cookie notice. The cookies we set are analytics cookies from Google Analytics 4, used to understand general site usage patterns. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or cookies to identify you personally.

You can decline cookies at any time through the cookie notice when you first visit the site, or by clearing your browser's cookies and revisiting the site.

Data Retention

Estimate requests you submit are received by email and retained in our inbox. We keep this information for as long as it is relevant to our business relationship with you. If you would like your information removed from our records, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at cornerstonepropainting@gmail.com or by phone at 437-242-3829. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information you submit. Our website is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are transmitted through EmailJS using encrypted connections. We do not store your form data on our website's servers.

Children's Privacy

Our services and website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us
