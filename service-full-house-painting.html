Full House Painting in Toronto — What It Really Involves

A full house repaint is not the same as painting a few rooms. It requires careful planning, staging of furniture across multiple rooms, and a coordinated crew that works efficiently without leaving any space half-done. Cornerstone Pro Painting has completed hundreds of full-house repaints across Toronto and the GTA since 2009, and our process is built around minimal disruption and maximum quality.

Full house painting typically covers every painted surface in the home: walls, ceilings, trim, doors, door frames, window casings, and baseboards. We coordinate the colour story across rooms so the transitions feel intentional — not like each room was painted by a different person on a different day. Whether you are refreshing your home before a sale or investing in a long-overdue update, a full house repaint delivers the most dramatic transformation available at the most cost-effective price per room.

Our Process — Step by Step

Step 1: Walkthrough & Quote — We visit the home, assess surface conditions in every room, and provide a written quote with a clear scope. No vague line items — you know exactly what's being painted, with what product, and how many coats.

Step 2: Colour Consultation — We help you develop a cohesive colour story for the full home — wall colours, trim colours, ceiling treatment, and any accent decisions — so everything flows together from room to room.

Step 3: Preparation — Furniture is moved and protected with heavy canvas drop cloths. Surfaces are cleaned, patches are filled and sanded, and any areas requiring primer are primed before any finish coat is applied.

Step 4: Painting — Ceilings are painted first, then walls, then trim and doors. Each room is completed fully before moving to the next. We maintain a clean work area throughout.

Step 5: Final Walkthrough — Before we leave, we walk every room with the homeowner, address any touch-ups, and leave the home cleaner than we found it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full house repaint take?

A standard 3-bedroom detached home takes 4–6 days with a full crew. Larger homes with 4–5 bedrooms typically run 6–9 days. We give you a specific timeline in the written quote.

Do I need to move all my furniture?

No. Our crew moves furniture to the centre of each room and protects it with drop cloths. We ask that you remove fragile items and clear the floors of personal belongings before we arrive.

What paint brands do you use?

We use Benjamin Moore Regal Select and Aura, Sherwin-Williams Emerald and Duration, and equivalent premium products depending on the surface and application. We never use contractor-grade paint on residential projects.

Can you match my existing paint colour?

Yes. Bring us a paint chip or we can take a colour sample from the wall and have it matched. We can also access paint company databases to find original formula codes.

How much does a full house repaint cost in Toronto?

A 3-bedroom detached home typically runs $5,000–$8,500. A 4–5 bedroom home runs $8,000–$13,000+. Price depends on ceiling height, surface condition, number of colours, and scope of trim work. We provide a written quote after the walkthrough.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry full general liability insurance and WSIB clearance. We are happy to provide certificates on request before any work begins.
