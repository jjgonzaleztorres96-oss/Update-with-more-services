Our Approach

Full House Painting in Toronto & the GTA

A full house repaint is one of the most effective investments you can make in your home. Fresh paint on every wall, ceiling, trim, and door transforms how a home feels — and when it's done right, it lasts. At Cornerstone Pro Painting, a full house project means a single dedicated crew, a detailed written scope, and a final walkthrough before we leave.

We handle full house repaints for detached homes, semis, townhouses, and condos across Toronto and the GTA. Our process is straightforward: we move and cover furniture, apply full surface preparation including sanding and priming where needed, complete all painting in sequence, and clean up completely before the final walkthrough. No shortcuts at any step.

How We Work

Our Process

01
Estimate & Colour Consultation

We start with a free on-site visit, a written itemized estimate, and colour guidance if needed. You'll know exactly what's being painted and with what products.

02
Surface Preparation

All surfaces are sanded, patched, and primed as needed. Furniture is moved and protected with drop cloths. Edges are taped precisely.

03
Painting in Sequence

We work room by room — ceilings first, then walls, then trim and doors — so nothing gets touched twice and paint edges stay clean.

04
Final Walkthrough

Before we pack up, we walk through the entire home with you to confirm every surface meets our standard. Any touch-ups are done on the spot.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About
Full House Painting

How long does a full house interior repaint take?

Most standard two-storey detached homes take three to five days. Larger homes or those with extensive trim, coffered ceilings, or multiple colours may take longer. We'll give you an accurate timeline during the estimate.

Do I need to move my furniture?

We move most furniture as part of the job. Very heavy pieces (piano, built-in shelving) should be moved in advance if possible. We protect all furniture and floors with drop cloths throughout.

What paint do you use for a full house repaint?

We use Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore exclusively — specifically their premium interior lines. We'll recommend the right sheen for each surface: flat or matte for ceilings, eggshell or satin for walls, semi-gloss for trim and doors.

Can you match my existing paint colours?

Yes. We can colour-match from a chip sample or identify original Benjamin Moore or Sherwin-Williams colours using our colour tools.

Do you paint ceilings?

Yes — ceilings are always included in a full house repaint. We apply flat white or your chosen ceiling colour with a consistent, even finish.
